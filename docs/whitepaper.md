# Propad Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** A Solana market where a new project starts on the same footing as everyone who might back it.

**Mission.** Build a pad that removes private head starts, keeps imitation off the board, and routes swap revenue back into the projects that earn it. Every figure the pad shows should be traceable to an outside source, and every external fact should live in one auditable record.

## Principles

1. **Level start.** No insider allocation at the opening bell. Creators enter at the same price as the crowd.
2. **Original names only.** Each submission is compared with existing listings before it can appear.
3. **Revenue feeds the loop.** Swap revenue is routed into open-market repurchases and permanent supply reduction.
4. **Figures in plain sight.** Market data comes from an outside aggregator, never from a hand-edited sheet.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, site and record board. Streaming market pulse online. Contract address, X and GitHub entries move from pending to stated once the team confirms them.

**Phase 2: Opening bell.** First listings under the level-start rule. Name screening goes live for every submission.

**Phase 3: The loop.** Swap revenue routing into repurchases and supply reduction, with the routing visible on the site.

**Phase 4: Open tooling.** Public documentation, integrations and community-run tools built on the record board.
